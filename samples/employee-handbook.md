# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

## Working hours

Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours you plan your own day.
Meetings are never scheduled on Friday afternoons; that time is reserved for focused work.

## Time off

Everyone gets 28 days of paid vacation per year, plus public holidays of the country they live in.
Vacation requests go through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for anything longer than
three days. Unused vacation carries over for one year, up to a maximum of 10 days.

Sick leave does not require a doctor's note for absences of up to three consecutive days. Tell your
manager in the team channel before 10:00 on the first day.

## Equipment

New starters choose a MacBook Pro or a ThinkPad X1 Carbon. Laptops are replaced every three years.
Each employee also has a one-time home office budget of 1,000 EUR for a desk, chair and monitor.
Keep the receipts: purchases are reimbursed through the expenses tool within 30 days.

## Learning budget

Every employee has 1,500 EUR per year for courses, books and conferences. Conference trips also
include two days of paid time that do not count as vacation. Ask your manager before booking; approval
is usually given within a week.
